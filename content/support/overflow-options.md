# What are my options when the included AI runs out?

Three, and you can switch anytime on the Billing page:

- **Cap-and-hold** (default) — paid AI pauses; readers keep getting free-model answers. Your
  maximum is your plan fee plus any fixed add-on-seat charges and any overflow already
  incurred this month.
- **Pay-as-you-go** — paid AI keeps running past your allowance, drawing your prepaid balance
  at your plan's member rate, up to a monthly cap you set.
- **Shutoff** — all AI stops (including free models) until next month's allowance arrives or
  you change the mode.

Good to know: the cap starts at one month of your allowance when you subscribe, and a plan
change resets it to one month of the new plan's allowance — your mode and any acknowledgment
carry over. A cap-and-hold pause isn't a funds problem, so topping up your balance doesn't
lift it; switching to pay-as-you-go or the next monthly reset does.
