# What if our published prices change after I subscribe — does my bill go up?

No. You keep the price you signed up at for as long as you stay on that plan. If we later
change our published pricing, your live subscription isn't moved — the price you agreed to is
the price you pay, and undoing a scheduled downgrade restores that same price.

Good to know: changing plans is a new purchase at that plan's then-current published price.
Add-on seats are the one line that tracks the catalog: an existing seat charge keeps its rate
until you next change your seat count or plan, when the current per-seat rate applies.
