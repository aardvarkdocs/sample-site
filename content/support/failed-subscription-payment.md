# What if a subscription payment fails?

Your plan enters a grace period while the card is retried, and everything keeps working — your
included AI, member rate, and seats stay in place. You get an email for each failed attempt so
you can update the card. If a retry hasn't succeeded within 14 days of the first failure, the
plan lapses to Free: paid AI returns to the pay-as-you-go rate, any unspent allowance ends, and
new invites are limited to Free's single seat (existing members keep their access). Your prepaid
balance is never used to cover a subscription payment.
