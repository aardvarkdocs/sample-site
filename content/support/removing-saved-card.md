# Can I remove my saved card while I'm on a subscription?

Yes. Removing it releases the card from your subscription and detaches it, so nothing keeps
charging a card you've taken off — and auto top-up switches off with it.

Good to know: with no card on file your next renewal can't be charged, which starts the
[failed-payment grace period](/support/failed-subscription-payment/). Add a replacement card
before your renewal date to avoid it.
