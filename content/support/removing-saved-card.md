# Can I remove my saved card?

Yes. Removing it detaches the card from your account, so nothing keeps charging a card you've
taken off — and auto top-up switches off with it. Your prepaid balance is untouched and keeps
serving usage until it runs out. Add a card again any time from the Billing page to top up or
re-enable auto top-up.
