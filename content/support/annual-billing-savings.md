# How does annual billing save money?

The discount is 20% off the platform fee; your included-AI allowance is funded at full value
every month either way (it's real usage, not a discountable line item). That works out to
about 12% off the yearly total on Pro ($1,046.40 instead of $1,188), 13% on Business
($3,638.40 instead of $4,188), and 15% on Enterprise ($28,200 instead of $33,000) — see
[plans and pricing](/pricing/). The allowance still resets monthly on annual plans, and add-on
seats bill yearly at 12× the monthly seat rate with no discount.
