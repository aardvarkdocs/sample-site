# How do seats work?

Seats cover your dashboard team — the **owners, admins, and members** who manage and edit
your docs. **Readers are always free and unlimited** on every plan. Each plan includes a set
number of seats (Free 1, Pro 5, Business 12, Enterprise unlimited), and on Pro and Business
you can buy **add-on seats** when you need more: **$10/seat/mo on Pro**, **$15/seat/mo on
Business** (annual plans bill seats at 12× the monthly rate, so the yearly total matches the
monthly one).

Good to know: the account owner occupies one seat, so Free's single seat is the owner's —
subscribe to invite teammates. A pending invite holds a seat until it's accepted or expires
after 7 days.
