# How does Enterprise billing work?

Enterprise is **priced per contract**. There's no published monthly price and no self-serve
checkout for it: [book a call](https://calendly.com/aardvarkdocs/new-meeting), tell us what you
need, and we'll quote the fee together with the included-AI allowance, seats, and member rate that
come with it. The Enterprise column on [plans and pricing](/pricing/) is where that quote starts,
not a rate card — a contract can move any of it.

Once it's signed we provision the plan on your account and bill it **separately**, with no card
on file for the subscription. Your dashboard then reflects your contract terms, including the
"max possible bill" for the plan you're on. Metered AI beyond your allowance works exactly as it
does on every other plan, at your contract's member rate.

Good to know: a contract plan is managed through us rather than self-serve — plan and seat
changes go through support — and cancelling it ends it immediately rather than at the end of a
paid period.
