# Feedback

Ways to tell a reader **what is happening** — the callouts, loaders, bars, rings, gauges,
and placeholders you reach for while something is in progress (or has just finished). Most
are a single Markdown tag with no setup; two of them — the top-of-page bar and the
toasts — are driven from JavaScript instead, and each shows a live demo you can click.

- [Callout](/components/feedback/callout/) — titled admonitions in four severities.
- [Loader](/components/feedback/loader/) — a spinner in three styles (oval, bars, dots).
- [Navigation progress](/components/feedback/nprogress/) — a thin top-of-page bar you drive from JavaScript.
- [Notification](/components/feedback/notification/) — a titled message card with a colored accent line.
- [Notifications](/components/feedback/notifications/) — a toast system you trigger from JavaScript; the call pops a message into a viewport corner.
- [Progress](/components/feedback/progress/) — a progress bar, single-value or multi-segment.
- [Ring progress](/components/feedback/ringprogress/) — a circular donut ring with one or more colored sections and a center label.
- [Semi-circle progress](/components/feedback/semicircleprogress/) — a half-circle gauge with a center label.
- [Skeleton](/components/feedback/skeleton/) — a shimmering placeholder for content that hasn't loaded.
